---
name: Reflection preservation boundaries
description: Product limits for reflection history and sprint access.
---

Do not consolidate historical duplicate reflections or introduce a one-submission-per-sprint constraint. Additions are supplemental, not a way to rewrite original answers, authorship, sprint assignment, or earlier additions. Teams are not a reflection privacy boundary.

**Why:** The user explicitly required historical submissions to remain intact and excluded duplicate cleanup, team-based privacy, and administrator editing of others' reflections.

**How to apply:** Preserve these limits when expanding reflection features or changing storage. A request to add history or access controls is not permission to clean up duplicate submissions or change initial submission requirements.
